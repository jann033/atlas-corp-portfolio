The opportunity

One workflow for both sides of a project

For clients

Describe a job with a guided brief, compare relevant specialists and follow agreed deliverables through visible milestones.

For freelancers

Present work in a useful portfolio, find suitable paid projects and see the fee and expected payout before committing.

Payment and payout flows will be developed with licensed partners. Cross-border availability is subject to partner agreements and legal validation.

Execution plan

Start focused. Expand on evidence.

01 / BUILD

Four-month development

Launch the web core in Uzbek and Russian, validate project workflows and confirm the legal and payment structure.

02 / PILOT

Uzbekistan first

Run a controlled pilot, measure paid projects, completion, repeat use and the actual cost of acquiring clients.

03 / EXPAND

Regional rollout

Add Android and iOS and assess Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan once demand and payout routes are confirmed.
