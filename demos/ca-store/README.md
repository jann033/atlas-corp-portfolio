# CA.store — interactive Mini App concept

Portfolio demonstration by ATLAS CORP / Djannik Aliev. This is a concept, not a claim of a commissioned or deployed CA.store integration.

Includes a product catalogue, product details, cart, illustrative instalment scenarios, account screens and two sample PDF documents. Product images and branding are embedded in the original demo; their respective owners retain their rights. Prices and financing examples are for demonstration.

No payment gateway, store backend or real contract signing is connected. Do not enter real financial or identity data.

Open index.html through any static HTTP server.
